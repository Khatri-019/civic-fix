# CivicFix real dataset card

Generated by `tools/finalize_dataset.py`. English only.

## Contents
- **436 complaint texts**: 376 from 94 photos (4 variants each) and 60 text-only.
- **Train 340 / test 96 rows.** The split is by photo (21 test photos), so all texts of a photo are in the same split. Text-only rows are split by row.
- **94 contradicting rows**: a complaint that the photo visibly disproves (exaggeration, wrong fault or wrong scale).
- **94 voice clips** (12.7 min) from 4 speakers (2 male, 2 female). 25 test rows have at least one recording.

## Label provenance
| label_source | rows | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| photo_label_inherited | 376 | Gemini wrote the text; category and severity come from the photo, which the team verified |
| seed_label_unchecked | 60 | text-only row; labels are the generation seed, and no person checked them |

The texts were **not individually reviewed** (Step 4 was skipped), so there is no inter-annotator agreement score.
4 contradicting texts that failed a manual read were rewritten (`text_source = claude_rewrite`).
Contradicting rows keep the photo's severity, so their severity label doesn't describe their text. Leave them out
of severity training and severity scoring.

## Speech recognition (word error rate, lower is better)
`app` = the app's Whisper setup (base, language auto-detected). `forced_en` = the same model forced to English.
`base_en` = Whisper's English-only model.

| group | clips | app | forced_en | base_en |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all clips | 94 | 12.5% | 12.5% | 12.2% |
| speaker M1 | 24 | 13.1% | 13.1% | 12.6% |
| speaker M2 | 24 | 8.8% | 8.8% | 8.8% |
| speaker F1 | 24 | 13.6% | 13.6% | 11.1% |
| speaker F2 | 22 | 14.3% | 14.3% | 16.5% |
| shared lines (same 11 texts) | 44 | 13.6% | 13.6% | 13.8% |

With 2 speakers per gender, speaker differences outweigh gender differences, so no gender effect is claimed.
Noisy clips used different texts from the quiet ones, so quiet vs noisy isn't a fair comparison.
